Visualization of Temporal Processing

Frame-level Speaker Embedding Before Temporal Enhancer

X-axis: Frame  |  Y-axis: Frequency (64-dim)

It can be observed that more than half of the feature maps show almost no variation over time, lacking frame-level conditioning capability.

Frame-level Speaker Embedding Enhanced by Temporal Enhancer

X-axis: Frame  |  Y-axis: Frequency (64-dim)

It can be observed that the enhanced feature maps all exhibit clear temporal structures. The ablation results also demonstrate that the addition of TE brings 0.7dB SI-SDRi improvement (ID2 over ID1), while only adding 0.06G MACs/s computational cost. These findings all validate the effectiveness of TE.
